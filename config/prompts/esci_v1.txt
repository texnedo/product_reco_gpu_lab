Rank the PRODUCT by how useful it is as a shopping result for the QUERY.

Prefer relationships in this order:
1. The product directly satisfies the query and all explicit constraints.
2. The product can replace the requested item but is not an exact match.
3. The product complements or is used together with the requested item.
4. The product is neither a match, substitute, nor meaningful complement.

A replacement is a substitute, while an accessory is a complement.
